Carlos Eduardo Tavares

Researcher in Quantum Computing and Programming Languages

About Me

I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science from a consortium of the Universities of Minho, Porto, and Aveiro, with my thesis titled "Foundations for Quantum Algorithms and Complexity". My research focuses on quantum computing, particularly the syntactic and semantic aspects of quantum programming languages and implicit complexity theory.

Research Interests

  • Quantum Computing and Algorithms
  • Programming Languages (Syntactic and Semantic Foundations)
  • Implicit Complexity Theory
  • Applications of Quantum Computing in Machine Learning
  • Formal Methods and Program Certification

Recent Work

I have worked on quantum hardware using linear optics, exploring its applications in optimization and machine learning. My current focus includes certifying quantum algorithms using tools like the ZX-calculus and Lov-Calculus, and analyzing their applicability to real-world problems.
