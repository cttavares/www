About Me

Hi! Welcome to my personal web page. I am a researcher in computer science, an IT professional, and an (invited) auxiliary professor at the University of Minho.

I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science on quantum algorithms and their applications. In recent years, my research has focused on quantum computational models based on optics. I am deeply interested in various topics surrounding computation and computational models, particularly quantum computing, quantum algorithms, and the physical phenomena that enable quantum advantage.
